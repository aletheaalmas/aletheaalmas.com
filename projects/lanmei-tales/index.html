Lanmei Tales

Overview

Lanmei Tales is personal window into my little family life in China. We live in different culture as my husband is Chinese and i'm Indonesian. I share daily moments of being Indonesian Muslim in China, raising a bilingual daughter. I share our moments in Instagram, Tiktok, and Youtube.

Journey

After i got laid off in 2025, i applied for jobs, but i always got an email saying "we decided not to move forward". I also learning new skills like coding, but its really difficult. It takes a lot of time and consistency to master it. And especially now, with AI everywhere, people just jump straight to AI. It's really hard to build that expertise, and it takes so much longer to get job in IT when compered to just using AI. But i'm still glad, because i already start to learning it. So, i have a basic understanding of what the fundamental are, even if i cant master it completely.

I was planning to keep going, i just dont want to stay at home and do nothing, i had to do something but "what?". My sister suggested why not create some content? At first, i was really unsure. I was embrassed. My first video actually use an AI voice. I have so many moments that captured on video and photo in my phone that actually can be shared as a good story but i never published them because i just to shy.

On top of that， i was also confused about the niche and format. I looked at some content from other people living in China and tested different ideas. Some of other account focusing on their relationship with their spouse, sharing their intimacy. I CANT do that, shy shy shy, and my husband doesen't really like being on camera either. There also some account sharing about marriage process like the documents and steps. I shared that too, but i felt like it was too focused on spesific group of people and wasn't really relatable to general audience. When i tested these videos my sister said "Yeah， its about documents again", and she could not relate. So i want to create content that isn't just for people who want to maddying Chinese person but for everyone can enjoy.

Result

In the end， there was one video that really took off. It hits more than 1 million views on TikTok and 500K on Instagram. In the video i was just yapping honestly about how i met my husband. And people love it. It turned out people really connected with personal and genuine side. So i thought okay i just be my self and share how i feel. What i feel here in China.

I managed to get 6000 followers in Instageam in two months and i'm so grateful. Also i got some brand deals in two months. I hope i can keep this up and stay consistent, and continue forward with it, because i think this could open up a lot of other opportunities for me.

Lanmei Tales is the real nuanced daily life in China, full of both challenges and beauty. Along the way i shared Mandarin phrases, clutural insights from my POV. Of course its not a fairy tales， it's a Lanemi Tales.
